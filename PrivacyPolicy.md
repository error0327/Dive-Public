# Dive Soundings Privacy Policy
Effective 23 November 2026

Obada Abu Alhiga publishes Dive Soundings (shown as Dive on some screens). For privacy questions or requests, contact error0327@gmail.com.

Dive stores your dive logs, visibility observations, saved preferences and forecast cache in the app’s private storage on your device. You can use Dive without creating an account. There is no advertising or analytics SDK in this version.

When Dive refreshes a forecast, it sends the coordinates of the coastal area you selected to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. Open-Meteo receives the connection IP address and request URL and can process them for technical operations, rate limiting and abuse prevention. The request identifies the app and the forecast area chosen from the catalogue. Details: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms. Alerts and widgets can refresh this information in the background. Dive does not request your device’s GPS location. Online maps and imagery load from map providers including Esri and GovMap. Their servers receive network information such as your IP address and the area or map tiles requested. Embedded map pages may load their own services. Each provider handles those requests under its own privacy policy.

Dive does not upload your private logs to the publisher. If you export your records, Android lets you choose the file destination. The export may contain your notes and observations. Copies saved outside the app remain under your control. If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request.

Delete individual records in Dive or clear the app’s storage in Android settings to remove locally stored information. Clearing storage also resets preferences. App data is excluded from Android backup. Delete exported files separately at the destination you chose.
